# Security policy

We take the security of offsteel and of our users seriously. Thank you for helping us keep them safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report security issues in public issues, discussions or pull requests.

Use one of these private channels:

1. **GitHub private vulnerability reporting.** Open the repository on GitHub, go to the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
2. **Email:** security@offsteel.com

Please include:

- the affected repository, component and version or commit;
- a description of the issue and its impact;
- steps to reproduce, a proof of concept or logs, if you have them;
- how you would like to be credited, if at all.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge new reports within 3 business days.
- We send a first assessment within 10 business days.
- We keep you informed while we work on a fix and agree with you on a disclosure date.
- We credit reporters in the release notes, unless they prefer to stay anonymous.

## Scope

This policy covers the repositories of the `offsteel` organization and the services at `offsteel.com` and its subdomains. Please do not run tests that degrade service for other users, access data that is not yours, or use social engineering or physical attacks.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith, follow this policy and give us reasonable time to fix an issue before any disclosure.
